# Sample sources

All recordings are public domain ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)), so no attribution is required. Each file is one note, named by the MIDI note it sounds. All were trimmed, faded out, converted to mono, level-matched and encoded as 96 kbps mp3.

- `bell/`: [Chromatic handbells 12 tones](https://freesound.org/people/radwoc/packs/20825/) by radwoc, from Freesound. Sounds 370002, 370000, 370003, 369997 and 370001; each file is cut to its first single strike.
- `xylophone/`: [Toy Glockenspiel](https://freesound.org/people/hollandm/packs/38756/) by hollandm, from Freesound. Sounds 693343, 693345, 693351, 693339, 693344, 693346 and 693352. Their filenames are three octaves below the sounding pitch, so `Glock-C3` is `84.mp3`.
- `whistle/`: Baroque Soprano Recorder (sustain) from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by Versilian Studios. A soprano recorder sounds an octave above its written note, so `SopRecorder_Sus_C4` is `72.mp3`. `SopRecorder_Sus_E5` (E6) is left out: it is overblown, with a second harmonic only 7 dB below the note. `74`, `76`, `78`, `91`, `94` and `96` start 16–166 ms into their recordings, once the recorder has settled on the note instead of squeaking on another, with a 40 ms fade-in to match the natural swell of the other notes.
